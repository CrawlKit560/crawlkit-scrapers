# CrawlKit Pricing

All CrawlKit Actors use **pay-per-result** pricing on [Apify](https://apify.com). You only pay for the data you get.

## How It Works

1. **No subscription required** — start with the free tier.
2. **Pay per result** — each item pushed to the dataset is one billable event.
3. **Volume discounts** — higher Apify subscription tiers unlock lower per-result prices.

## Pricing Tiers

Apify offers subscription tiers that reduce the per-result cost:

| Tier | Monthly cost | Typical discount |
|------|-------------|-----------------|
| **Free** | $0 | Base price |
| **Bronze** | $49 | ~15% off |
| **Silver** | $149 | ~25% off |
| **Gold** | $499 | ~40% off |

See [apify.com/pricing](https://apify.com/pricing) for current subscription details.
